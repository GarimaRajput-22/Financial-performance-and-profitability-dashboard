# Financial Performance & Profitability Dashboard

## Overview
An Excel dashboard that analyses revenue, expenses and profitability from Jan 2023 to Jul 2026. It gives management a clear view of where money comes from, where it goes, and how profit is changing.

## KPIs
- Total Revenue: ₹[26,052.M]
- Total Expenses: ₹[3,406.M]
- Net Profit: ₹[4,645.M]
- Profit Margin: [76.764]%

## Dashboard Components
- *Revenue Over Time*: line chart of monthly revenue
- *Expense By Category*: bar chart of spending by category
- *Profit By Region*: donut chart of profit share by region
- *Top 5 Expenses*: column chart of the biggest expense categories
- *Revenue vs Expense*: yearly comparison (2023–2026)
- *Net Profit Trend*: area chart of profit over time

## Filters (Slicers)
- Department
- Region (Central, East, North, South, West)
- Channel
- Expense Category
- Date timeline

## Key Insights
1. *Strong growth with healthy profitability*: revenue grew over the period while profit stayed positive, so expansion is turning into sustainable earnings.
2. *Performance is concentrated in key drivers*: the West region and the Sales department contribute the most revenue.
3. *Salaries are the largest expense*: management should protect growth while controlling cost efficiency here.

## Recommendations
- Focus on strengthening the West region and Sales department
- Review salary costs and other top expenses
- Replicate West-region success in weaker regions

## Tools Used
Microsoft Excel (PivotTables, PivotCharts, Slicers, Timeline)

## How to Use
1. Open the workbook in Excel.
2. Use the slicers to filter by department, region, channel or expense category.
3. Drag the date timeline to select a period.
4. All charts and KPIs update automatically.
